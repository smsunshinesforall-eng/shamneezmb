Solution Architect Decision Guide

1. Video Surveillance — DeepinView (AI) · ColorVu (low-light color) · AcuSense (false alarm reduction) · Thermal (perimeter)

2. Recording & Storage — NVR (IP) · DVR (analog) · CVR (enterprise) · pStor (scalable clustered storage)

3. Access Control — Face/fingerprint terminals · Card readers · Mobile credentials (Bluetooth/NFC/QR) · Multi-door controllers

4. Video Intercom — Door stations · Indoor monitors · Multi-tenant systems · Hik-Connect mobile integration

5. Alarm & Intrusion — AX PRO (wireless) · AX HYBRID · Intrusion panels · PIR/perimeter sensors

6. Software — HikCentral Professional (enterprise) · iVMS-4200 (local) · Hik-Connect (mobile) · OpenAPI/SDK

7. AI & Analytics — Guanlan AI models · DeepinView · AcuSense · AcuSeek · ANPR · Face recognition

8. Specialized — Radar (security/industrial/care) · LiDAR · X-Ray · UVSS · Acoustic sensors

9. Display — LED video walls · Monitors · Digital signage · Content management

10. Robotics — Security patrol · Logistics AGV/AMR · Disinfection · Service robots

How to read this reference

  • Definition — plain-language description of what the product line is and does.
  • Use case — the shortest tag for the primary application.
  • When to choose — trigger conditions that push you toward this product.
  • Fit — S/M/L boxes ticked = natural fit at that deployment scale.
  • Cost — LOW = entry-level, single-unit; MED = professional, multi-unit; HIGH = enterprise, multi-site or specialized. Actual cost is deployment-dependent.
  • Products marked with * are legacy or being phased out.
