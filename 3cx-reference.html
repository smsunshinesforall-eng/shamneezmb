How to read this reference

  • Definition — plain-language description.
  • Use case — primary business job.
  • When to choose — trigger conditions.
  • Fit — natural organizational size.
  • Cost — indicative footprint only; confirm current commercial pricing with 3CX/partner.

Why 3CX?

  • Open-standard IP communications platform.
  • Hosted, self-hosted and on-premise deployment choices.
  • System-based licensing rather than traditional per-user pricing.
  • Integrated phone system, contact center, digital channels, video and AI.
  • Strong CRM and Microsoft 365 integration options.
  • Integrated AI agents, transcription and call intelligence.
