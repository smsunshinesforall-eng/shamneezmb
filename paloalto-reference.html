How to read this reference

  • Definition — plain-language description of what the product is and does.
  • Use case — the shortest tag for the primary job.
  • When to choose — trigger conditions that push you toward this product.
  • Fit — S/M/L boxes ticked = natural fit at that org size.
  • Cost — LOW = entry-level; MED = professional; HIGH = enterprise or carrier-grade. Hardware + subscription costs vary by throughput and deployment.
  • Products marked with * are legacy or being phased out.
