How to read this reference

  • Definition — plain-language description of the product.
  • Use case — the shortest tag for its primary job.
  • When to choose — trigger conditions that push you toward it.
  • Fit — S/M/L ticked = natural fit at that org size.
  • Cost — LOW = few $K/yr; MED = tens of $K/yr; HIGH = $100K+/yr or hardware-heavy.
  • Products marked with * are legacy, end-of-life or rebranded.
