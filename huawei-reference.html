Solution Architect Decision Guide

1. ICT Infrastructure — 5G-A (mobile) · F5G-A (optical) · NearLink (short-range) · CloudEngine (switching) · NetEngine (routing)

2. Computing — Ascend (AI) · Kunpeng (ARM) · TaiShan (ARM servers) · FusionServer (x86) · Atlas (AI products)

3. Cloud — ECS (compute) · OBS (storage) · GaussDB (database) · ModelArts (AI platform) · Pangu (AI models)

4. Digital Power — FusionSolar (PV+ESS) · Grid Forming ESS · LUNA S1 (home storage) · EV charging

5. Automotive — ADS 5 (autonomous driving) · HarmonySpace 6 (cockpit) · XMC (chassis) · LiDAR/radar (perception)

6. Consumer — Mate/P/nova/Y (phones) · MateBook (laptops) · MatePad (tablets) · WATCH/FreeBuds (wearables)

7. Software — HarmonyOS (device OS) · openEuler (server OS) · HMS Core (app services) · DevEco (IDE)

8. AI — Pangu (foundation models) · ModelArts (platform) · MindSpore (framework) · CANN (compute stack)

How to read this reference

  • Definition — plain-language description of what the product line is and does.
  • Use case — the shortest tag for the primary application.
  • When to choose — trigger conditions that push you toward this product.
  • Fit — S/M/L boxes ticked = natural fit at that deployment scale.
  • Cost — LOW = entry-level; MED = professional; HIGH = enterprise or carrier-grade. Actual cost is deployment-dependent.
  • Products marked with * are legacy or being phased out.
