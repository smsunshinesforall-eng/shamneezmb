01 / Brand foundation

Shared values.
Distinct personalities.

MagicMind AI Labs is the technology identity. MagicMind Crafts is its handmade crafts subsidiary. A shared name and recurring emblem connect both brands while colour, imagery and tone define their individual worlds.

02 / Logo language

The meaning
behind the marks.

A suggested reading of the visual elements in your artwork, followed by practical rules to keep the identities recognisable.

AI Labs · imagination connected

  • Human profile: places people and imagination at the centre of technology.
  • Connected mesh: suggests intelligence, learning and linked ideas.
  • Star and sweeping curve: suggest discovery, movement and the promise of magic.
  • Layered blues: create depth, energy and a clear technological character.

Crafts · imagination made tangible

  • Shopping bag and gift tag: communicate gifting and a crafted retail experience.
  • Hand and heart: suggest care, personal attention and handmade value.
  • Recurring MagicMind emblem: provides a visible link to the parent identity.
  • Teal and blush: balance creative energy with warmth and softness.

05 / Visual direction

A family resemblance,
without sameness.

Use the same disciplined spacing and navy typography across both brands. Let imagery, shapes and accent colours express each brand’s character.

AI Labs visual language

  • Photography: people working with technology, thoughtful interaction and clear details. Use real project imagery when available.
  • Graphics: fine connecting lines, restrained nodes and flowing curves inspired by the logo.
  • Layout: structured grids, generous margins and strong hierarchy. Keep diagrams labelled and uncluttered.
  • Icons: simple rounded line icons with consistent stroke width. Avoid mixing unrelated styles.

Crafts visual language

  • Photography: real handmade pieces, hands at work, material textures and thoughtful gift presentation.
  • Graphics: soft arches, tag-like frames and occasional sparkle details inspired by the artwork.
  • Layout: calm editorial compositions, light backgrounds and room for the product to stand out.
  • Styling: natural light and simple props. Let texture show; avoid filters that misrepresent product colours.
Use an 8 px spacing rhythm: 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64. Keep decorative patterns away from reading areas. Outside the logos, use gradients sparingly and keep typography on solid, high-contrast surfaces.

07 / Brand voice

Human at heart.
Intentional in every word.

Both brands should sound imaginative, warm and clear. Express the benefit in plain language and support factual claims with evidence.

AI LABS

Confident. Curious. Approachable.

Explain technology through its human purpose. Use direct language, define unfamiliar terms and avoid exaggerated promises.

Suggested message
“Bring a fresh perspective to what technology can make possible.”

CRAFTS

Warm. Thoughtful. Expressive.

Describe the care, detail and feeling behind handmade work. Be specific about materials and processes when those details are known.

Suggested message
“A handmade detail to make the moment feel personal.”

Keep “Experience Magic!” and “The Gift Destination” as shown in the supplied logos. Other phrases in this guide are proposed campaign copy. Avoid unsupported claims such as “the best”, “guaranteed results”, “sustainable” or “fully handmade” where the exact product or service does not support them.
